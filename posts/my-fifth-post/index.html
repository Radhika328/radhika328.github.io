Reflection Blog On Presentation

May 11, 2026

The presentation on Inclusive Terminology presented by another group is very good. I liked how the group focused on important social issues in technology, and how language influences people’s experiences and feelings. Their presentation was creative and well structured. I liked that they included a real example such as “whitelist/blacklist and master/ slave”, this shows how certain words in technology can unintentionally create bias. 

I agree with most of the ideas presented in their topic. Technology is used by many people from different cultures and genders, so it is important to use respectful and inclusive language. Using inclusive terminology helps us to create a comfortable environment, where everyone feels valued and respected.

However, I think some part of the presentation could have been improved. Some slides had too much design and also the text font is very less. So during presentation time that text makes it harder to read. A few slides also contained long sentences and grammar mistakes that affected clarity. The group could have used simpler wording, larger fonts to make the content easier for the audience to follow during the presentation.

Overall, the topic is meaningful and relevant to today’s technology industry. The presentation encourages the audience to think about how language and communication in technology can impact society.
